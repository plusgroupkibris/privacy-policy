Privacy Policy

Car Dealer Tycoon: Auto Lot (bundle com.fiveparmak.otogaleri) by 5parmak. Last updated: 5 October 2026.

Summary

You do not need an account, email address or phone number to play. The game stores your progress on your device. If online features are available, the game talks to our server using an anonymous player ID. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads in this version and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

What the game stores on your device

Save data: money, cars, upgrades, staff, settings and language. It stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

What is sent to our server

When online features (leaderboards, clans, weekly leagues, auctions) are used, the game creates an anonymous player account and sends:

  • an anonymous player ID and a secret access token generated by our server (not linked to your Apple ID, name or device identifiers);
  • the gallery name you choose (shown publicly to other players in leaderboards and clans, so do not use your real name);
  • the city you pick in the game, your level, scores and season results, clan membership and actions (such as joining a clan or entering an auction);
  • basic technical data every internet request contains (IP address, time, app version). We use it to operate and secure the service.

We use this data only to run these features: ranking players, matching clans and keeping the competition fair. It is not used for advertising or tracking and it is not shared with third parties other than our hosting provider that processes it on our behalf.

Purchases

In-app purchases, when offered, are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Contact us if you believe a child has used online features and we will delete the account.

Retention and deletion

Online data is kept while the account is active. To delete your online data, email destek@5parmak.site with your player ID (shown in the game profile) and we will delete it within 30 days. See how to delete your data. Local data is removed by uninstalling the game.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the UK GDPR) you may ask for access, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing. Email us at the address below.

Advertising

This version contains no advertising. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy and our App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Changes

If we change this policy we publish the new version here with a new date.
