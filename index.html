5Parmak ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy rights of our users ("users" or "you"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application Car Dealer Tycoon: Trade & Wash.

1. Information We Collect

Car Dealer Tycoon is designed primarily as an offline single-player simulation game. We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as your real name, physical address, or phone number.

  • Gameplay & Progress Data: Game saves, progress, inventory, garage upgrades, and virtual coins are stored locally on your device.
  • Device and Usage Information: Anonymous diagnostic and device telemetry (such as device model, OS version, performance framerates, and crash logs) collected automatically to optimize gameplay.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob) may collect anonymous advertising IDs (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, subject to App Tracking Transparency consent) to serve relevant advertisements.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our game displays rewarded and interstitial advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob / Unity Ads). These providers may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to measure ad effectiveness and personalize ads where permitted by law.

You can review Google's privacy policy here: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Car Dealer Tycoon is rated for general audiences (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us at support@5parmak.com, and we will promptly remove the information.

4. Data Retention and Security

All game save files reside locally on the user's mobile device storage. If you delete or reinstall the application, local save progress may be reset unless backed up via native device cloud services (Google Drive / Apple iCloud).

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or limit ad tracking in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
  • Data Deletion: You can completely erase all local data at any time by clearing application data in device settings or uninstalling the application.
